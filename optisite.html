SLB OptiSite vs AVEVA

OptiSite is not an AVEVA competitor at the SCADA or historian layer; it competes with AVEVA Predictive Analytics, Unified Operations Center and Process Simulation for the asset-performance tier above the data, and it runs on Cognite Data Fusion, the same engine Schneider Electric is buying to fold into AVEVA CONNECT.

What OptiSite is

Certain OptiSite is "a set of cloud-based facility, equipment, and pipeline solutions" launched by SLB's Production Systems division on 24 January 2025, pairing digital twins with AI to give operators oversight of an entire production network (SLB tech spotlight; SLB Q1 2025 8-K). It is available on the Delfi platform and runs on the Lumi data and AI platform, which integrates Cognite Data Fusion; SLB calls this the Operations Data Foundation (SLB article, 2025; Q3 2025 8-K).

Certain Wells and gathering networks are not OptiSite's scope. That is the sister product, OptiFlow production assurance (flow assurance, production analytics, advisors, intervention); Lumi connects the two (Cognite partner page). Avocet remains a separate on-premise production data management system in SLB's catalogue.

Commercial model

Certain No page states OptiSite pricing, licensing unit or whether it is subscription or outcome-based. SLB classifies Delfi, Lumi and Petrel as "sold as subscriptions or perpetual licenses, mostly recurring" and Digital Operations revenue as "repeatable, with a portion recurring"; Digital ARR was US$1.0bn at December 2025 and US$1.04bn at June 2026 (Q2 2026 8-K). Likely OptiSite is sold as a Lumi/Delfi subscription plus SME-led services, given the "ready within 12 weeks" and "subject-matter-expert-led services" language. Against AVEVA's published Flex mechanics (credits, named users, tag tiers), SLB's model is opaque, which is an argument a buyer can use either way.

Middle East and named customers

Certain The only named OptiSite customer is ONGC India, where Lumi is deployed enterprise-wide and OptiFlow and OptiSite "will be deployed to support and enhance production operations" (Q1 2026 8-K). SBM Offshore signed an exclusive digital alliance for FPSOs in October 2025 with OptiSite enabled by Cognite Data Fusion (Offshore). An unnamed Houston independent is scaling it across offshore US assets.

Certain No Gulf NOC is named as an OptiSite customer. What exists is adjacent: ADNOC's AiPSO programme is "powered by the SLB Lumi data and AI platform and leveraging Cognite Data Fusion", eight fields initially and all 25 by 2027, with no OptiSite mention (SLB, Nov 2025); KOC has a five-year KwIDF support contract, a five-year MSA including "generative AI for production optimization" and four Innovation Factori collaborations under the Jurassic gas digital field, none naming OptiSite (SLB 8-Ks, 2025–26). Likely The "Middle East facility" in SLB's launch article is one of these NOCs. Against that, AVEVA's named Gulf references are KOC West Kuwait and ADNOC Panorama (see Middle East).

The Cognite question

Certain Three facts collide. SLB's Operations Data Foundation is Cognite Data Fusion under an exclusive 2022 arrangement for the energy enterprise data solution (Businesswire, 2022). Schneider Electric agreed on 30 June 2026 to buy Cognite for US$3.1bn and fold its data foundation and Atlas AI into AVEVA CONNECT (Schneider). The 2021 SLB–AVEVA agreement to integrate Agora and Delfi with PI System has no public renewal or mention since (SLB, 2021). Likely Once the Cognite deal closes, OptiSite's data layer is owned by AVEVA's parent; either SLB's exclusivity survives as a licence term, or Lumi has to be re-platformed. Neither outcome is public yet, and it is the single most useful question to ask an SLB account team in a KOC tender.

What it means for a Landmark / DFS seller

  • Likely OptiSite plus OptiFlow is the closest thing to a full well-to-sales competitor for DFS, because SLB covers the subsurface and well layers AVEVA cannot. The AVEVA comparison on this page is therefore partly a proxy: in a KOC consolidation tender the realistic field is SLB (Lumi/OptiFlow/OptiSite), AVEVA (CONNECT/PI/UOC) and Halliburton (DFS), with Cognite inside two of the three.
  • Certain OptiSite is cloud-only in every public description. For an NOC that keeps production data in-country and on-prem, that is a specification point AVEVA (hybrid Flex) and DFS can both meet and SLB has not publicly demonstrated for OptiSite.
  • Certain SLB has no published metering model; AVEVA does. A buyer asking for price transparency gets tag tiers and named users from AVEVA and a services-led quote from SLB.
  • Certain Verdantix's 2024 APM quadrant assessed AVEVA, AspenTech, Baker Hughes, Honeywell and GE Vernova; SLB and Cognite were not in it (Verdantix). Independent analyst coverage of OptiSite was not found.
