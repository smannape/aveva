AVEVA products and Middle East pricing

AVEVA is a wholly owned Schneider Electric software unit (100% since January 2023) headquartered in Cambridge, UK, with 6,000+ staff and a portfolio that absorbed Wonderware, Citect and OSIsoft PI. It now sells one platform, CONNECT, with every legacy product repositioned as an application on it.

Read me first

AVEVA publishes no price list, in the Middle East or anywhere else; everything priced here is reconstructed from Flex programme mechanics, public procurement records and tender awards, each tagged by how solid it is. Treat this as a living reference: the portfolio section is stable, the pricing section decays within a year.

Two cautions apply to the whole reference.

First, AVEVA sells through Schneider Electric and a distributor layer in the Gulf, so any price point from a US or UK public record is an upper-bound reference, not a GCC quote.

Second, AVEVA has been moving customers from perpetual licences to Flex subscriptions since 2023, so older perpetual figures found online overstate what a new buyer would pay up front and understate the lifetime cost.

Headline facts

  • US$13.5–14.5 per Flex credit On a committed three-year deal; US$19–21 on a small one, with 3–5% annual escalation inside the term. The only hard pricing anchor. Certain

  • No Kuwait office Kuwait is served from Dubai through partners; offices confirmed in Dubai, Al Khobar and Dhahran. Likely
