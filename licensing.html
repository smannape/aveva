Licensing and commercial model

Since 7 March 2023 every new licence for AVEVA's major engineering and operations products is sold as an AVEVA Flex subscription denominated in credits; perpetual is officially off the menu, though it is still obtainable in practice (AVEVA press release, Mar 2023).

How Flex works

All Certain, from the Flex datasheet, the Flex partner deck and two US municipal order forms listed under Pricing.

  • The credit is the single currency. A customer commits to an annual allotment, invoiced yearly, and spends it on any mix of on-prem, hybrid or cloud products during the term.
  • Two charge types: fixed rates (a product burns N credits per month, e.g. PI Data Infrastructure 10K tags = 274 credits/month) and usage rates on CONNECT (metered by active users, concurrent users, TB stored, data streams per day), with volume discounts built into the rate card.
  • Terms: Standard Flex 1–3 years; Flex Enterprise and Enterprise Plus 3 years minimum, with Premium or Elite support (10% or 20% consulting discount, named exec sponsor at the top tier).
  • Unused credits expire at the end of the order-form term with no carry-forward; overdraft must be topped up within 45 days; top-up credits are priced 5–10% above the committed rate; orders are non-cancellable and non-refundable.
  • AVEVA's marketing claim is up to 65% lower upfront cost versus perpetual. Read that as a cash-flow claim, not a lifetime-cost claim: a three-year Flex deal with 3–5% annual escalation typically crosses the old perpetual-plus-maintenance cost between year four and year six. Likely
  • Perpetual-licence holders were offered a trade-in credit against subscription from October 2022; the credit value is never stated publicly (AVEVA PR, Oct 2022).

Legacy perpetual model, support fees and activation

Certain Under perpetual licensing the annual Customer FIRST fee is "calculated as a percentage of the list price of AVEVA software at your facilities or sites"; the percentage is never published, lapses attract a reinstatement fee that grows with the lapse, and version upgrades are only included if you were on a current version when you enrolled. Guessing A competitor blog puts the fee at 20% of licence with 5–8% annual escalation (MachineCDN, 2026); treat as unverified.

Certain Activation runs through the AVEVA Enterprise License Server: a licence is permanently linked to the server machine, activated online against AVEVA's activation server or offline by exchanging sync files; Flex entitlements arrive as an XML licence file, normally within 48 hours of a request, and renew automatically (InSource; Factory Software TN049). Credits are pooled across every site on the contract, and the burn-down resets each contract year.

Where the money is heading

Schneider's filings show the transition working: AVEVA ARR grew 15% in FY2024, 12% in H1 2025 and 12% in FY2025, recurring revenue is close to 85%, and perpetual and maintenance revenue is declining as customers migrate to Flex credits; the Middle East was named as a double-digit growth region for AVEVA in H1 2025 (Schneider H1 2025; Schneider FY2025).
