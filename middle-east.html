Middle East

AVEVA runs the Gulf as direct-for-NOCs from Dubai and Dhahran, with a thin distributor layer for everything below enterprise scale; Kuwait has no AVEVA office and is covered from Dubai through partners.

Footprint

Offices confirmed in Dubai (regional HQ since 2004), Al Khobar (2015) and Dhahran (May 2024, consolidating Saudi operations under VP MEA Nayef Bou Chaaya) (Khaleej Times; Cambridge Network; MEA TechWatch).

Likely No Kuwait, Qatar or Oman office exists; Kuwait is served from Dubai through partners (Omnix International was named for Kuwait, Qatar and KSA in 2016, source).

Certain Schneider's own commentary credits the Middle East, and the UAE specifically, with double-digit AVEVA growth in FY2024 and H1 2025.

Confirmed Middle East customers

KOC West Kuwait Certain

System Platform, InTouch HMI and Workflow Management across eight facilities (gathering centres, booster stations, water facilities), GC-16 for Minagish at 120,000 bpd target; AVEVA claims up to 5% revenue improvement and 10% opex reduction (AVEVA KOC case study, 2020).

ADNOC Panorama Certain

Command centre on Unified Operations Center, System Platform and InTouch OMI, plus Unified Supply Chain and Predictive Analytics; 10m+ tags, 120+ dashboards, claimed US$60–100m savings (AVEVA ADNOC story).

Qatar Power (IPWP) Certain

AVEVA PI System, “almost US$300,000”, tag count not stated (AVEVA Qatar Power case study, 2022).

Saudi Aramco Likely
