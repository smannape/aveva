ADNOC: AVEVA, SLB and the Halliburton counter

At ADNOC, AVEVA owns the enterprise visualisation and historian layer through Panorama, SLB captured the production-optimisation layer in November 2025 with AiPSO, and Halliburton's only visible foothold is RoboWell through AIQ. OptiSite has not been named at ADNOC by anyone; the counter runs through AIQ, not around it.

Research as of 25 Oct 2026 from ADNOC, AVEVA, SLB and Halliburton press releases, SEC filings and trade press. No contract value for AVEVA or SLB at ADNOC is public. The "Halliburton counter" section is positioning logic, tagged Likely or Guessing, and should be checked against account knowledge this research cannot see.

AVEVA at ADNOC: Panorama

Certain Panorama is ADNOC's Digital Command Centre on the 37th floor of its headquarters, established in 2017 and inaugurated by Sheikh Mohammed bin Zayed in late 2018, with a 50-metre screen fed by what ADNOC calls 250,000 real-time data points (ADNOC, 2021; Gulf News, 2018). ADNOC credits it with more than US$1bn of business value since inception and it won the OTC 2021 Distinguished Achievement Award.

Likely AVEVA's position at ADNOC is strongest at the bottom (PI in every OpCo) and the top (the wall), and weakest in the middle: its predictive analytics compete with Honeywell Forge on rotating equipment and with AIQ-led Neuron 5, and nothing public shows AVEVA in the well or reservoir domains.

SLB at ADNOC: AiPSO and ENERGYai

Certain AiPSO, the AI-powered Production System Optimization platform, was announced at ADIPEC on 3 November 2025 for eight fields initially and all 25 onshore and offshore fields by 2027, covering thousands of wells and hundreds of processing facilities from wellhead to processing. It runs on SLB's Lumi platform with Cognite Data Fusion and ADNOC's own ML models, and is positioned as complementing AIQ's ENERGYai (SLB; Gulf News; MEES). No field names, contract value or production figure is public; Delfi, OptiFlow and OptiSite are not mentioned.

Certain Separately, SLB joined AIQ's ENERGYai in July 2025 to co-deploy agentic subsurface workflows (geology, seismic, reservoir modelling) on Lumi, after a test on 15% of the data from two fields showed a 10× faster seismic agent (World Oil). ENERGYai itself is a US$340m, three-year AIQ contract across more than 28 producing fields (Offshore Technology). SLB's Q4 2025 remarks name the ADNOC partnership and double-digit 2025 growth in the UAE, Iraq and Kuwait (SLB). ADNOC Drilling's Turnwell JV (SLB 30%, Patterson-UTI 15%) adds a drilling-side digital channel (World Oil).

How SLB can position OptiSite at ADNOC

Likely SLB does not need to sell OptiSite into ADNOC; it needs to switch it on. AiPSO already puts the Lumi and Cognite data foundation across all 25 fields by 2027, and OptiSite runs on exactly that foundation. The plausible sequence, built from what SLB has said elsewhere:

  1. Extend AiPSO from wells to facilities. AiPSO's stated scope ends at processing facilities; OptiSite's Asset Health and Process Optimization modules are the next step on the same data. SLB's Houston reference moved from "unified visualisation from well to sales" to OptiSite in months (SLB, 2025).
  2. Attack the predictive-maintenance middle. That layer is already split between Honeywell Forge (2,500 rotating items) and AVEVA/AIQ Neuron 5 (700 compressors, pilots). OptiSite's launch trial claimed 88% of failures predicted with up to 21 days' warning at an unnamed Middle East facility; SLB will present that against both incumbents.
  3. Use the Cognite entity in Abu Dhabi. Cognite opened an Abu Dhabi company in August 2025 (Cognite), giving the data foundation local presence before the Schneider acquisition closes.
  4. Pipeline integrity as the wedge. Neither AVEVA nor AIQ has a public pipeline-integrity analytics product at ADNOC; OptiSite's inspection-alignment and corrosion-forecasting module has no incumbent to displace.
  5. Agentic layer via Tela. SLB's Tela assistant already sits on Lumi and was co-awarded with OptiSite at OTC Asia 2026; it slots under ENERGYai's agent framework rather than competing with it.

Guessing The counter-pressure SLB faces: once Schneider owns Cognite, ADNOC's production-optimisation data layer is owned by AVEVA's parent, and ADNOC's procurement will ask whether AiPSO is still vendor-independent. That is the open question nobody has answered publicly.

Halliburton at ADNOC today

Certain Halliburton's visible digital position is RoboWell, the autonomous well-control solution built with AIQ and live at ADNOC Onshore's NEB field since October 2023 (30% less gas-lift gas, 50% fewer well movements), extended offshore to NASR in July 2024 (10 wells, 300+ planned) and in commercial use across hundreds of wells by May 2025 with up to 5% production gain (Halliburton, 2023; World Oil, 2024; Offshore, 2025). Since July 2024 Landmark's iEnergy hybrid cloud is AIQ's global channel for RoboWell (Halliburton). ADNOC's own NASR release credits AIQ and Thamama only; Halliburton is unnamed.

Certain No DecisionSpace 365, integrated-asset-modelling or digital-twin contract with ADNOC was found, and no Halliburton earnings release in 2024–2026 mentions ADNOC; UAE appears only for completion tools, iCruise Force and well-construction activity (Halliburton Q2 2026). On the non-digital side Halliburton holds a share of the 2022 US$1.83bn directional-drilling and LWD framework (JPT).

The Halliburton counter

Likely Halliburton cannot out-platform SLB at ADNOC: Lumi is in 25 fields by 2027 and AIQ's ENERGYai is a US$340m programme already under way. The counter is to make RoboWell and DFS the well-and-reservoir physics layer inside those platforms, which is where AIQ, not SLB, holds the keys.
