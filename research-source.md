## Read me first

AVEVA publishes no price list, in the Middle East or anywhere else; everything priced below is reconstructed from Flex programme mechanics, public procurement records and tender awards, each tagged by how solid it is. Treat this as a living reference: the portfolio section is stable, the pricing section decays within a year.

Two cautions apply to the whole doc. First, AVEVA sells through Schneider Electric and a distributor layer in the Gulf, so any price point from a US or UK public record is an upper-bound reference, not a GCC quote. Second, AVEVA has been moving customers from perpetual licences to Flex subscriptions since 2023, so older perpetual figures found online overstate what a new buyer would pay up front and understate the lifetime cost.

## Licensing and commercial model

Since 7 March 2023 every new licence for AVEVA's major engineering and operations products is sold as an AVEVA Flex subscription denominated in credits; perpetual is officially off the menu, though it is still obtainable in practice ([AVEVA press release, Mar 2023](https://aveva.com/en/about/news/press-releases/2023/aveva-commits-to-subscription-transition-to-foster-flexible-industrial-innovation)).

**How Flex works** (all [Certain], from the [Flex datasheet](https://www.aveva.com/content/dam/aveva/documents/datasheets/Datasheet_AVEVA_FlexSubscription_22-09.pdf.coredownload.inline.pdf), the [Flex partner deck](https://cdn.mediavalet.com/eunl/content/zJAKrNwXNUmvNddZkzQJdg/HJWovbPlpk-HcD6u6n_u2w/Original/AVEVA%20Flex%3A%20Eliminate%20traditional%20barriers%20to%20adoption%20with%20technical%20and%20commercial%20flexibility.pdf) and two US municipal order forms linked in the next section):

- The credit is the single currency. A customer commits to an annual allotment, invoiced yearly, and spends it on any mix of on-prem, hybrid or cloud products during the term.
- Two charge types: fixed rates (a product burns N credits per month, e.g. PI Data Infrastructure 10K tags = 274 credits/month) and usage rates on CONNECT (metered by active users, concurrent users, TB stored, data streams per day), with volume discounts built into the rate card.
- Terms: Standard Flex 1–3 years; Flex Enterprise and Enterprise Plus 3 years minimum, with Premium or Elite support (10% or 20% consulting discount, named exec sponsor at the top tier).
- Unused credits expire at the end of the order-form term with no carry-forward; overdraft must be topped up within 45 days; top-up credits are priced 5–10% above the committed rate; orders are non-cancellable and non-refundable.
- AVEVA's marketing claim is up to 65% lower upfront cost versus perpetual. Read that as a cash-flow claim, not a lifetime-cost claim: a three-year Flex deal with 3–5% annual escalation typically crosses the old perpetual-plus-maintenance cost between year four and year six. [Likely]
- Perpetual-licence holders were offered a trade-in credit against subscription from October 2022; the credit value is never stated publicly ([AVEVA PR, Oct 2022](https://www.aveva.com/en/about/news/press-releases/2022/aveva-delivers-more-flexibility-to-customers-of-aveva-pi-system/)).

**Where the money is heading.** Schneider's filings show the transition working: AVEVA ARR grew 15% in FY2024, 12% in H1 2025 and 12% in FY2025, recurring revenue is close to 85%, and perpetual and maintenance revenue is declining as customers migrate to Flex credits; the Middle East was named as a double-digit growth region for AVEVA in H1 2025 ([Schneider H1 2025](https://webdisclosure.com/press-release/schneider-electric-epa-su-half-year-2025-results-kPCEGR5e3pT); [Schneider FY2025](https://echanges.dila.gouv.fr/OPENDATA/AMF/ECO/2026/02/FCECO081066_20260226.pdf)). [Certain]

**Perpetual is not dead.** [Guessing] A March 2025 r/SCADA post says AVEVA still grants perpetual terms if the customer refuses subscription enough times ([thread](https://redlib.hackliberty.org/r/SCADA/comments/1j15b9l/aveva_scada_pricing_model/mfuct4s/?context=3)). [Likely] US resellers still list tag-based InTouch 2023 R2 SKUs with no subscription wording, and AVEVA's own InTouch page still prints MSRPs. Expect the same in the GCC for NOCs with capex-only budgets.

## What this means for a Landmark / DFS seller

AVEVA is already inside KOC at the surface-facilities layer, so the realistic competitive question is not "AVEVA or DFS" but which one owns the integration tier above SCADA and PI when the KwIDF estate is consolidated; AVEVA's 2026 moves (CONNECT, Cognite, TwinThread) are aimed squarely at that tier. [Likely]

| Layer | AVEVA position | DFS / Landmark position | Read |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Field instrumentation, PLC, DCS | None; Schneider Electric parent sells hardware | None | Neutral |
| SCADA / HMI | System Platform, InTouch, Plant SCADA; already at KOC West Kuwait | Not a product | AVEVA owns it; do not compete here |
| Historian / data infrastructure | PI System, the de facto NOC historian; CONNECT data services | DFS reads from PI and CygNet | Partner, never replace |
| Integration and context layer | CONNECT, knowledge graph, Twin builder (2026–27), Cognite data foundation post-close | DFS integrated asset model, well and network models | Head-on overlap from 2027 |
| Subsurface-to-surface engineering workflows | Weak: Unified Engineering is plant design, not reservoir or well modelling | Core: nodal analysis, network, forecasting, intervention | DFS advantage; AVEVA has no reservoir physics |
| Predictive analytics on equipment | Predictive Analytics (PRiSM), TwinThread | Well-performance ML | AVEVA stronger on rotating equipment; DFS stronger on wells |
| Planning and scheduling | Unified Supply Chain is refinery and petchem only | Field development and production planning | Different domains |

**Positioning lines that survive scrutiny** [Likely]:

- AVEVA prices by tags, users and streams on Flex; a tag-count-indexed subscription grows linearly with an NOC's instrumentation. A workflow-priced DFS proposal can be sold as decoupled from tag growth.
- AVEVA has no petroleum-engineering physics. Anything requiring IPR/VLP, network solving or reservoir coupling sits outside its catalogue, which is why its own ADNOC story stops at visualisation and supply chain.
- Flex credits expire at term end with no carry-forward; for a 5+1-year programme, that is a real procurement argument against a credit-based competitor.
- Cognite inside CONNECT is the threat to watch: Cognite Data Fusion already sells contextualisation to Aramco and ADNOC-class buyers, and after closing it becomes a Schneider/AVEVA product with a Gulf field force. Expect it in the next KwIDF tender's competitive set.

**Lines to avoid**: claiming AVEVA is being spun out, retired or merged away (no evidence); quoting any GCC price for AVEVA as fact (none exists publicly).
