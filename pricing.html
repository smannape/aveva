Worked example: a 100K-tag PI estate on Flex

Likely Scaling the Lakeland rates linearly (AVEVA's dynamic rate plans mean the real per-unit rate should fall at this volume, so treat this as a ceiling): 10 × PI Data Infrastructure 10K = 2,740 credits/month; HA node for the same = 1,020; PSA for a third-party integration layer = 990; 100 named PI Vision users = 366; CONNECT visualisation for 100 users = 500. Total about 5,600 credits/month, 67,000/yr, which at US$13.75 is roughly US$925,000 per year before distributor margin and before any System Platform, Operations Control or Predictive Analytics credits. A 300K-tag estate on the same arithmetic is about US$2.8M/yr. These are the numbers a Gulf NOC quote through AITS or Peritus would start from, uplifted 10–20%.

How to use this for a GCC estimate

Likely Multiply the burn rate by US$14–16/credit for an enterprise term and by US$19–22 for anything under about 2,000 credits a year, then add 10–20% for the distributor margin and support uplift that a Gulf quote through AITS or Peritus carries.

A NOC-scale PI estate (for KOC's order of magnitude, several hundred thousand tags across System Platform, PI and Predictive Analytics) lands in the low-to-mid millions of US dollars per year on Flex; the 10M-tag ADNOC Panorama estate is well above that but no figure is public.
